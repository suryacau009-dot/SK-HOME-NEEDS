Our Story

A Small Family Shop, Serving Our Neighbourhood

SK Home Needs started as a small counter shop selling detergents and cleaning essentials to families in our own neighbourhood. What began with a handful of products has grown into a trusted local stop for everyday home care — but we've kept the same values: fair prices, honest advice, and fast, friendly service.

We personally choose every product on our shelves — from laundry powders to mops and brushes — so that every household nearby can keep a clean home without stretching the monthly budget. No corporate call centres, no confusing apps: just message us, and we'll sort it out.
